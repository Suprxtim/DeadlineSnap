🤖 SETUP TELEGRAM BOT

  1. 📱 Open Telegram app on your phone or desktop
  2. 🔍 Search for @BotFather (official bot)
  3. 💬 Send the /newbot command
  4. ✏️ Follow prompts to name your bot (e.g., "MyDeadlineBot")
  5. 🔑 BotFather gives you a TOKEN — copy it
  6. 📋 Paste the token in the "Bot Token" field above
  7. 🔗 Click the t.me/YourBotName link BotFather sent
  8. ▶️ Press START button to open chat with your bot
  9. 🔌 Click "Connect Telegram" button in extension
  10. 🆔 Extension will auto-detect your Chat ID
  11. ✅ Test by clicking "Send Test Message"

🔒 Your bot is private — only you can send/receive messages.
